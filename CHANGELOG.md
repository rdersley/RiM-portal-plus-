# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Retail inMotion edition

First release of Retail inMotion Portal+, split from the Marketplace edition of Portal+ as of 20 October 2026.

- Own Forge app for the Retail inMotion work site; no Marketplace licensing.
- Retail inMotion UI kit (`@retailinmotion/ui`) and branding.
- Reads Retail inMotion Asset Manager's portal snapshot (`retailinmotion.asset-manager.portal`) and Retail inMotion Smart Approval Manager's approvals.
- Backup & restore of a project's Portal+ settings, used to move the work site's settings from the previous Portal+ app.
- Includes everything Portal+ had at the split: help-center layout, Services grid with portal-group import, page layout presets, first-visit view, More menu, companion and extra menu tabs.
